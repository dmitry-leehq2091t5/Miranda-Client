### 🔮 About

**Miranda** is a modern Minecraft client built around three things: **performance**, **customization** and **utility**. No bloat, no noise — just a clean, fast and good-looking client that does what you need and stays out of your way.

---

### ✨ Features

- **🚀 Ultra Performance** — higher FPS, fewer lag spikes
- **🎨 Deep Customization** — modern UI, themes, glowing accents
- **🛡️ Built-in Modules** — utility & QoL mods out of the box
- **🌐 Cross-Version Support** — smooth compatibility
- **🧩 Lightweight Core** — minimal footprint, fast startup
- **🔄 Auto-Updates** — always on the latest version

---

### 🛠️ Installation

```text
1 ─ Download the latest release
2 ─ Move Miranda to your desktop
3 ─ Launch - Select profile - Play
```

---

### ⚙️ Configuration

- **Keybind** → `Right Shift` *(rebindable)*
- **Config Folder** → `.minecraft/miranda/config.json`
- **Auto-save** → on by default

---

### 🎮 Keybinds

| Action | Default |
|:--|:--|
| Open GUI | `Right Shift` |
| Toggle HUD | `H` |
| Toggle Sprint | `Ctrl` |

---

### 📦 Modules

- **Combat** — reach, hitbox, autoclicker
- **Movement** — sprint, safe walk, step
- **Render** — ESP, tracers, nametags
- **HUD** — FPS, CPS, coordinates, armor
- **Misc** — auto-reconnect, chat tweaks

---

### 🎨 Themes

Pick a preset or build your own:

- 🌌 **Midnight** — deep purple glow *(default)*
- ❄️ **Frost** — cool blue accents
- 🔥 **Ember** — warm orange highlights
- ⚫ **Mono** — pure black & white
